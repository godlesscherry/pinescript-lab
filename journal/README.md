# Journal

Ongoing research log and high-level notes. Use this to capture ideas before they become a strategy or backtest.

## trade-research-log.md

- **Ideas** — Strategy concepts, indicator ideas, market hypotheses.
- **Quick notes** — What you tested, what worked, what to try next.
- **Links** — References to strategies in `strategies/` or backtests in `backtests/`.

Keep entries short and date them so you can trace how research evolved. When an idea becomes a concrete strategy, create a folder under `strategies/` and link to it from the log.
